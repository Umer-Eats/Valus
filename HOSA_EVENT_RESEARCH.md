# HOSA event tool mapping

Reviewed October 19, 2026 against the current [HOSA guidelines](https://hosa.org/guidelines/) and [digital upload guidance](https://hosa.org/competitive-event-digital-uploads/).

Scope: all 64 regular secondary-division events used by this high-school app. Middle-school-only events, recognition programs, and supplementary academic tests are separate programs and are not annual-event choices.

All events have Rules and Lessons. Practice Tests follow a written-test requirement. Skills Video Grader supports events where students perform a hands-on skill. Online Mock Procedure supports practical skill demonstrations and parliamentary meeting procedure. Speech Grader supports events where students present, give a speech, interview, or debate. Clinical Specialty keeps its recorded-skill grader; its live presentation does not have a Speech Grader tab. Online Upload Grader is included wherever the guideline specifies an upload, including administrative-only accommodation forms; those exceptions are explicitly labeled. Tools remain Coming soon. These are preparation-tool mappings, not claims that HOSA offers these tools.
